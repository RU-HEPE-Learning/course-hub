# HED3505 Pilot — Privacy Notice

## Public areas

Public repositories and the Interactive Learning Lab may contain:
- course instructions;
- templates;
- de-identified instructional examples;
- approved case data.

They must not contain:
- student IDs;
- identity mappings;
- private contact information;
- personal health information;
- official grades;
- confidential school information.

## Learner work

Student-specific work is private by default and must use a designated private repository or other approved private portfolio space.

## Interactive Learning Lab

The current lab is static-first, requires no login, and does not send learner answers to a mandatory backend.

## Reporting a privacy concern

Pause the affected workflow. Preserve the minimum evidence needed to diagnose the issue. Do not repost the affected content in a public issue or repository.
